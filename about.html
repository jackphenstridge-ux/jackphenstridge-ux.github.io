About

I'm Jack. Originally from the UK, now living in Ann Arbor. I came to design later than most, after a career in Human Machine Interface engineering. Over time I realized the part I cared about most was the human side of that equation. After years of self-teaching and bootcamps, I made the switch properly to UX and Product Design.

I'm currently a UX Designer at Criteo, working in Retail Media across UI and API design. Lately that's expanded into front-end work too, building with AI tools directly rather than handing sketches off and hoping they survive translation.

Outside of work I enjoy trail and ultra running, and I'm currently training for my next race. I have a passion for wildlife and landscape photography, which rewards patience and gives me a chance to sit still. I'm also a father, and that shapes how I see almost everything else here. It's hard to stay detached from how your work affects people once you're responsible for someone who can't yet advocate for themselves.

I'd love to work together on something great.
